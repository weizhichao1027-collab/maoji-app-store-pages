写信给支持 Email support

反馈时请写上设备型号、iOS 版本、App 版本、所在页面和复现步骤。 Please include device model, iOS version, app version, the screen you were on, and the steps to reproduce.

今日 Today

打开 App 后，先看照顾舱、今日打卡和待办。完成喂食或事项后点完成。疫苗、驱虫、体检到期会显示在这里。需要本地提醒时，在「我的」打开通知权限。

After you open the app, start with the care desk, today’s check-in, and tasks. Mark feeding or a task done when you finish it. Due vaccines, deworming, and exams show here. For local alerts, allow notifications under Me.

记录 Records

用几秒记下食欲、饮水、便便和精神。异常时补次数、持续时间和照片。体重可以另看趋势。时间线是给自己和医生看的事实，不是诊断。

Log appetite, water, stool, and mood in a few seconds. When something is off, add counts, duration, and a photo. Weight has its own trend. The timeline is a fact sheet for you and the clinic, not a diagnosis.

宠物 Pets

先建一只猫或狗的档案：过敏、慢病、常用药、喂食偏好和照顾备注。疫苗本、化验单可以从相册选照片，相机会保持关闭。头像也只从相册选。

Start with a cat or dog file: allergies, conditions, usual meds, feeding notes, and care remarks. Vaccine books and lab sheets come from the photo library. The camera stays closed. Avatars are picked from the library too.

摘要 Summaries

就诊前生成 7、14 或 30 天摘要。它只整理你已经写下的记录，不会下诊断。可以导出 PDF 或图片，发给家人或带到医院。

Before a visit, build a 7-, 14-, or 30-day summary. It only arranges notes you already wrote. It does not diagnose. Export a PDF or image for family or the clinic.

临时照看 Temporary care

在宠物页打开临时照看清单，只包含这一只宠物的过敏、喂食、备注和未完成事项。可分享文本或 PDF。

From the pet page, open the temporary-care list. It includes only that pet’s allergies, feeding notes, remarks, and open tasks. Share as text or a PDF.

我的：备份、语言、隐私 Me: backup, language, privacy

  • 完整备份是 zip，含文字和照片。请自己存到文件或电脑。没有云同步，删掉 App 且未导出，数据会丢。
  • A full backup is a zip of notes and photos. Save it yourself to Files or a computer. There is no cloud sync. Delete the app without an export and the data is gone.
  • 文本导出只有健康记录文字，不含照片文件。
  • Text export is health-record text only. It does not include photo files.
  • 界面语言可跟随系统，或在「我的」指定 13 种语言之一。
  • The interface can follow the system, or you can pick one of 13 languages under Me.
  • 删除宠物或记录后，数据会先隐藏。可在「我的」永久清理，或恢复前再确认。
  • Deleted pets or records are hidden first. Purge them under Me, or confirm before you restore.

照顾助手 Care helper

助手在本机用关键词整理你写的观察，生成就医准备和观察要点。它不是线上问诊，不会给出处方或剂量。

The helper sorts the words you typed, on this device, into visit-prep and watch points. It is not an online consult. It will not give a prescription or a dose.

常见问题 Common questions

为什么到期没有提醒？ Why did a due item stay silent?
到 iOS 设置 › 通知 › 毛孩子，打开允许通知。若曾点过不允许，App 内无法自己打开。打开后再回「我的」同步一次提醒。 Go to iOS Settings › Notifications › Pet Care Notes and allow alerts. If you had tapped Don’t Allow, the app cannot turn this back on by itself. After you allow it, return to Me and sync reminders.
换机后记录在哪？ Where are my notes after I change phones?
毛孩子不提供 iCloud 或服务器同步。请先在旧设备导出 zip，再在新设备「我的」里导入。旧版纯 JSON 备份仍可导入，只是没有照片文件。 There is no iCloud or server sync. Export a zip on the old device, then import it under Me on the new one. Older JSON-only backups still import, but they have no photo files.
照片为什么没带上？ Why is a photo missing?
照片只在你主动从相册选择后，才复制进 App 本机容器。如果备份时提示有照片没写进 zip，恢复后这些图可能缺失。导入后也会提示未恢复的附件数量。 A photo is copied into the app container only after you pick it from the library. If a backup warns that some photos were not written into the zip, those images may be missing after restore. Import also reports how many attachments did not come back.
演示数据能不能当病历？ Can I treat demo data as a real chart?
不能。演示数据写在本机正式库里，只供审核预览，带「演示」或 Demo 标记。可一键清除。日常请建自己的档案。 No. Demo data is written into the real on-device store for App Review only. It is marked 演示 or Demo and can be cleared in one tap. Build your own file for daily use.
有没有 Android 或网页版？ Is there Android or a web app?
没有。当前只提供 iPhone。家庭协作和账号也没有做。 No. This version is iPhone only. There is also no household sharing and no account.
它能诊断或建议用药吗？ Can it diagnose or suggest a dose?
不能。摘要和助手只整理你写下的内容。用药、处方和诊断请询问线下兽医。 No. Summaries and the helper only arrange what you wrote. Ask a veterinarian in person about medicine, a prescription, or a diagnosis.

紧急情况不要等 App Do not wait on the app in an emergency

呼吸困难、抽搐、尿闭、误食、中毒、大量出血、持续呕吐：请尽快联系线下宠物医院或急诊。毛孩子不能替代兽医。

Trouble breathing, a seizure, blocked urine, possible ingestion, poisoning, heavy bleeding, or ongoing vomiting: contact a pet clinic or emergency service. Pet Care Notes does not replace a veterinarian.
