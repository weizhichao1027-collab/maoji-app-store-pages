iPhone 上的宠物照顾笔记本 A pet-care notebook on iPhone

今天该做什么 What to do today

打开就看见待办和打卡。记下食欲、饮水、便便和精神，再把最近记录整理成医生可读的就诊摘要。数据默认只留在这台 iPhone。 Open it and see today’s tasks and check-in. Log appetite, water, stool, and mood, then turn recent notes into a clinic-readable visit summary. Records stay on this iPhone by default.

  1. 今日 Today

    待办、逾期、健康打卡，和下一次疫苗或驱虫。

    Tasks, overdue items, the daily check-in, and the next vaccine or deworming.

  2. 记录 Notes

    食欲、饮水、排便、精神、呕吐、体重和用药痕迹，留在时间线里。

    Appetite, water, stool, energy, vomiting, weight, and medication traces on a timeline.

  3. 摘要 Visit

    把 7、14 或 30 天的记录整理成医生可读的材料，导出 PDF 或图片。

    Turn 7, 14, or 30 days of notes into a clinic-readable packet. Export a PDF or image.

记下刚刚发生的事 What just happened

时间线留下可给医生看的线索。异常时可以补次数、持续时间和照片。毛孩子不猜测病因，只保留你写下的事实。

A timeline the clinic can read. When something is off, add counts, duration, and a photo. Pet Care Notes does not guess a cause. It keeps the facts you wrote.

一只猫的档案 One pet’s file

过敏、慢病、常用药和照顾备注放在一起。疫苗、驱虫、体检和化验单照片也在同一只宠物下面。猫和狗都能建档。

Allergies, conditions, usual meds, and care notes sit together. Vaccines, deworming, exams, and lab-sheet photos stay under that pet. Cats and dogs both work.

交给临时照看的人 Hand off for a day

请人照看时，可以交出一份只含这只宠物的清单：过敏、喂食偏好、未完成事项。能分享文本或 PDF。

When someone else steps in, share a list for this pet only: allergies, feeding notes, and open tasks. Share as text or a PDF.

界面有 13 种语言 The interface is in 13 languages

本机优先，换机自己带走 On this iPhone, until you export

含照片的 zip 备份 Zip backup with photos

完整备份是 zip，含文字和照片。存到文件、电脑或你自己的云盘。毛孩子不会替你上传。

A full backup is a zip of notes and photos. Save it to Files, a computer, or your own drive. The app does not upload it for you.

本地提醒 Local reminders

授权后，疫苗、驱虫、体检和照顾事项会用系统本地通知。关闭通知后不再提醒。

After you allow it, vaccines, deworming, exams, and tasks use local system notifications. Turn them off in iOS Settings to stop alerts.

照顾助手只整理关键词 The helper only sorts keywords

助手在本机把你写的观察收成就医准备笔记。它不提供诊断、处方或剂量建议，也不能替代兽医。

The helper organizes your notes on-device into visit-prep points. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or suggest a dose, and it does not replace a veterinarian.

医疗边界 Medical boundary

毛孩子只用于宠物照顾记录和就医准备。遇到呼吸困难、抽搐、尿闭、误食、中毒、大量出血或持续呕吐，请尽快联系线下宠物医院或急诊。

Pet Care Notes is for care notes and clinic prep. For trouble breathing, a seizure, blocked urine, possible ingestion, poisoning, heavy bleeding, or ongoing vomiting, contact a pet clinic or emergency service.
