A pet-care notebook on iPhone

What to do today

Pet Care Notes is a free iPhone app on the App Store for people looking after a cat or dog. It keeps today’s tasks, ten kinds of health notes, and clinic-ready summaries on this device. There is no account, cloud sync, or advertising. The Chinese name is 毛孩子.

Open it and see today’s tasks and check-in. Log appetite, water, stool, and mood, then turn recent notes into a clinic-readable visit summary. Records stay on this iPhone by default.

  1. Today

    The care desk, the four-item check-in, overdue and due tasks, and the next vaccine or deworming. Mark a task done, or open it to change the date.

  2. Notes

    Ten types: appetite, water, stool, urine, vomiting, energy, weight, unusual signs, medication traces, and remarks. Open a row to edit it. Weight has its own trend.

  3. Visit

    Turn 7, 14, or 30 days of notes into a clinic-readable packet. Export a PDF or image. Facts only. No diagnosis.

Who it is for, and who it is not

Pet Care Notes is an on-device care ledger. It is not a consult, a community, or a shop.

For

  • Someone looking after a cat or dog who wants to see today’s work
  • A visit where a time-ordered packet would help
  • People who will not create an account or upload a file to a developer server
  • A day when someone else steps in and needs a list for one pet

Not for

  • An online diagnosis, a prescription, or dose advice
  • A household account, live multi-user sync, or iCloud sync
  • Android, a web app, or a pet community
  • Treating the app as a medical device or an official chart

What just happened

A timeline the clinic can read. When something is off, add counts, duration, and a photo. Open a record to change it. Pet Care Notes does not guess a cause. It keeps the facts you wrote.

One pet’s file

Allergies, conditions, usual meds, feeding notes, and care remarks sit together, with microchip id, insurance, and clinic contact. Vaccines, deworming, exams, and other medical notes stay under that pet. Health and medical notes can both take a photo from the library. Cats and dogs both work.

Hand off for a day

When someone else steps in, share a list for this pet only: allergies, conditions, usual meds, feeding notes, open tasks, and a note not to change medicine on their own. Share as text or a PDF. The other person does not need the app or an account.

On this iPhone, until you export

Zip backup with photos

A full backup is a zip of notes and photos. Save it to Files, a computer, or your own drive. The app does not upload it for you, and there is no iCloud sync. Older JSON-only backups still import.

Local reminders

After you allow it, vaccines, deworming, exams, and tasks use local system notifications. Turn them off in iOS Settings to stop alerts. If alerts are off, the app will not interrupt you on every launch.

The helper only sorts keywords

The helper organizes your notes on-device into visit-prep points. It does not go online, call an external model, diagnose, prescribe, or suggest a dose, and it does not replace a veterinarian.

How to start

  1. 1

    On iPhone, open the App Store and search for Pet Care Notes or 毛孩子, or use the store link on this page. Free. Needs iOS 17.

  2. 2

    Open Pets and add a cat or a dog. The app creates common care tasks for that type.

  3. 3

    Start each day on Today. Before a visit, open Summaries and build a 7-, 14-, or 30-day packet.

Common questions

For full how-to and troubleshooting, see support.

What is Pet Care Notes?
Pet Care Notes is a free iPhone app on the Apple App Store for people looking after a cat or dog. It keeps today’s tasks, health notes, and clinic-ready summaries on the device. The Chinese name is 毛孩子. There is no account, cloud sync, or advertising.
Does Pet Care Notes cost money?
No. It is free, with no in-app purchases and no ads. It needs an iPhone and iOS 17 or later.
Where is the data stored?
Pet files, tasks, notes, and photos stay on this iPhone by default. The developer has no cloud copy and there is no iCloud sync. Export a zip with photos before you change phones.
Is there Android or a web app?
No. This version is iPhone only. There is also no household sharing and no account.
Does it replace a veterinarian?
No. Summaries and the helper only arrange what you wrote. They do not diagnose, prescribe, or suggest a dose. For an emergency, contact a pet clinic or emergency service.
What is the Chinese name?
The Chinese home-screen name is 毛孩子. The Chinese store name is 毛孩子宠物健康记录. The English store name is Pet Care Notes. The former name 毛迹 is no longer used.

Medical boundary

Pet Care Notes is for care notes and clinic prep. For trouble breathing, a seizure, blocked urine, possible ingestion, poisoning, heavy bleeding, or ongoing vomiting, contact a pet clinic or emergency service.
